Website Planning Document

Course: WDD 131 | Project: Ward Executive Secretary Resource Guide

Site Name

Proposed Name: LDS Ward Executive Secretary Digital Resource Guide

Reason for Choice: I currently serve as a Ward Executive Secretary within the Benin City Nigeria Uzebu Stake. Because I perform these administrative tasks every week, I chose this name to establish a clear, practical handbook. It leverages my direct, personal experience and organizational skills to build a dedicated platform that helps me and other church secretaries manage our callings smoothly and effectively.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide an informational, digital handbook designed to streamline the administrative duties of Ward Executive Secretaries in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, with a tailored focus on local units like the Uzebu Stake. The site will function as a central hub featuring step-by-step checklists for Sunday block meetings, Bishopric appointment scheduling workflows, Ward Council agenda templates, and optimization tips for managing digital ward calendars.

Target Audience Scenarios

A newly called or existing Ward Executive Secretary visiting the site might ask the following questions to help manage their calling:

  • Scenario 1: What specific tasks do I need to complete before, during, and right after Sunday block meetings to keep things running efficiently?
  • Scenario 2: Where can I find a structured template or checklist to help the Bishop coordinate and track his weekly interview schedule and Ward Council agendas?

Color Schema

The selected color schema establishes an orderly, clean, and reverent atmosphere fitting an administrative Church resource guide:

  • Primary Color (Deep Royal Blue - #002D62): Used for the main page header accents, section borders, and primary headings (H1, H2, H3) to communicate structure, organization, and official administrative leadership.
  • Secondary Color (Muted Gold - #D4AF37): Used for decorative borders underneath headings and accent highlights to denote calling importance and divine stewardship.
  • Text Dark Color (Dark Slate Blue - #2C3E50): Applied to all body paragraphs and list components to ensure high legibility while remaining softer and more modern than harsh solid black text.
  • Background Light (Off-White - #F8F9FA): Sets a crisp, light, distraction-free backdrop for the entire content wrapper.
