Project plan

A practical guide for a meaningful calling

This plan outlines a digital handbook to help ward executive secretaries stay prepared, organized, and supportive of their bishopric.

Site Name

WS

Ward Secretary Desk

Proposed domain: wardsecretarydesk.org (availability not checked)

I chose this name because it is short, easy to remember, and clearly describes a helpful workspace for ward executive secretaries. It reflects my weekly service in a ward in the Benin City Nigeria Uzebu Stake and the practical experience I hope to share. This is an independent resource, not an official Church website.

Site Purpose

Ward Secretary Desk will be an informational handbook for Ward Executive Secretaries of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, with examples informed by local units such as those in the Benin City Nigeria Uzebu Stake. It will provide step-by-step Sunday meeting checklists, a guide to coordinating the Bishop’s appointments, reusable Ward Council agenda templates, and practical tips for keeping ward calendars organized with digital tools. The goal is to help new and experienced secretaries serve smoothly and reliably.

Audience Scenarios

These questions represent needs a ward executive secretary may bring to the site:

  • What should I prepare before, manage during, and follow up after the Sunday block meetings?
  • How can I organize the Bishop’s interview appointments and communicate schedule changes clearly?
  • Is there a simple Ward Council agenda template I can adapt for our next meeting?
  • What is a reliable way to keep the ward calendar up to date and prevent scheduling conflicts?

Color Schema

The palette uses calm, professional colors to make an administrative resource feel clear, welcoming, and easy to scan. These colors are also applied throughout this planning document.

  • Deep navy — #123B5D: Page headings, navigation, logo mark, and primary accents.
  • Warm gold — #D6A84F: Decorative rules, small highlights, and emphasis accents.
  • Warm paper — #F5F3EC: Page background to create a soft, comfortable reading surface.
  • Charcoal — #24313A: Paragraph and list text for strong readability.

Typography

  • Georgia: Used for the page title, section headings, and site name to add a warm, established character.
  • Arial: Used for body text, navigation, labels, and controls for clear reading across common devices.
